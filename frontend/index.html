    PROJECT NOTES

    Waste Collection
    Route Optimizer

    This is a working educational prototype on a simulated 25-junction road network. It minimizes the total round-trip distance for one vehicle serving every selected stop.

    Try it

    Choose your pickup places, calculate a route, then change a waste load or close a road and calculate again. Over-capacity and disconnected routes are rejected.

    Python version

    From the project folder, run python run.py and open http://127.0.0.1:8000. The local app calculates routes in Python; this local edition sends calculations to its Python backend. No external packages or API key required.

    CSV format

    Use a header of node,name,kg. Nodes range from 0 to 24. Up to 10 unique stops; exclude the selected depot. Import replaces your stop list only after validation.

    Scope

    All roads are simulated, bidirectional, and have fixed distance weights. No live traffic, emissions estimates, GPS, time windows, or multiple vehicles. Repeated road segments are allowed. Passing a stop does not count as servicing it until its scheduled visit. Data stays in this browser; nothing is uploaded.

    SDG 11: better urban waste collection.
    SDG 13: less travel could reduce fuel use; measure actual consumption to validate.

    YOUR FIRST COLLECTION ROUND

    A little guide
    to getting started.

    You do not need to know any coding. Think of this as planning a truck’s daily round: pick where it starts, choose the bins to collect, and find a shorter way around.

    Your first route

    Choose Plan a collection route, tap the places that need a pickup, then press Find shortest route. Follow the arrows or select one part of the trip under Follow the trip.

    Type weights directly, use ±10 kg for pickups, or ±100 kg and capacity presets for the truck. Clear map removes pickups and road closures while keeping your truck settings.

    1. Choose the starting point

      The depot is where the truck starts and finishes. Choose it under Truck starts & ends here. Truck can carry is the maximum waste the truck can carry, in kilograms.

    2. Add the places to collect from

      Choose Plan a collection route, then tap a + on the map to add a pickup with a starting estimate of 40 kg. Tap a selected place again to remove it. Edit its name and load in the stop list. Alternatively, expand Add a stop using a form, choose a location and press + Add stop. You can add up to 10 stops.

      Edit names and waste amounts directly in the stop list. The × removes a stop. Your list order is the original route used for comparison.

    3. Find a shorter route

      Press Find shortest route. The app checks that the truck can carry the full load and reach every stop, then finds the shortest round trip on this fictional network.

      After changing stops, capacity, the depot or roads, press Optimize again. Blank distance cards mean the plan needs a fresh calculation.

    4. Read the map and results

      D marks the depot. Filled green circles are collection stops; their numbers show the visit order for the selected route. Pale + circles are available places to add a pickup. Arrows show travel direction. Choose one leg under Follow the trip to separate overlapping journeys. Distances are listed in kilometres in the pickup order.

      Before: the stop-list order. Best route: the best order found. You save: the reduction compared with the original. A result of 0% is fine: your original order may already be shortest.

    5. Try a road closure

      Expand More options and turn on Edit road closures, then click a road segment. A dashed road is closed. Click it again to reopen it, and optimize again to see the effect. Turn closure editing off before selecting new stops.

    6. Take your collection sheet

      Scroll to Your pickup order to see the optimized visit order, waste loads and distance between stops. Click Download stop list to download it for Excel or another spreadsheet app.

    What if I see an error?

    Over capacity: increase capacity if appropriate, or remove some stops from this round. Keep waste amounts realistic.

    Unreachable stop: road closures have cut off a required location. Reopen roads or change the stops.

    Depot already used: remove that location from the collection-stop list before choosing it as your depot.

    Invalid load or name: use a positive waste amount and a name of 1–80 characters.

    Can I import my own stop list?

    Yes. Under More options, choose Import CSV. Use the header node,name,kg, followed by one stop per row. Node IDs range from 0 to 24; use unique IDs and exclude your chosen depot.

    node,name,kg
0,Market bins,65
24,Garden bins,80
4,Station bins,50

    These are the fictional network’s junction IDs, not street addresses. A valid import replaces your current stop list. Files must be under 100 KB with 1–10 stops. The exported collection sheet has a different format and is not an import template.

    Can I share or save my plan?

    Copy link copies this local app address, not your stop list. A localhost address works only on the computer running the server. Export your collection sheet to share the calculated route.

    The app saves edits in this browser when local storage is available. On reopening it restores a valid saved plan; invalid saved plans start with an empty plan. Private browsing or clearing browser data can remove your plan.

    How do I run the Python version?

    Extract the project ZIP, open a terminal in the project folder, and run python run.py (or python3 run.py), then open http://127.0.0.1:8000. Python 3.10 or later is required. No API key or extra packages are needed.

    A quick note about this prototype

    The map is a simulated road network, not live city streets or GPS. The app plans one vehicle’s round and measures distance savings. It does not measure real fuel use or emissions. Click SDG 11 + 13 ⓘ to explore the project’s sustainability goals.
